IMD Rainfall Classification Standards

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) classifies rainfall based on percentage departure from the Long Period Average (LPA):

% Departure = ((Actual/Forecast Rainfall - LPA) / LPA) × 100

A. All-India National Standards:

  • Excess: Departure > +10% above LPA.
  • Normal: Departure within ±10% of LPA.
  • Deficient: Departure -10% to -29% of LPA.
  • Scanty / Severe Drought: Departure ≤ -30% of LPA.

B. Meteorological Subdivision Standards:

  • Excess: Departure ≥ +20% above subdivision LPA.
  • Normal: Departure within -19% to +19% of subdivision LPA.
  • Deficient: Departure -20% to -59% of subdivision LPA.
  • Scanty / Drought: Departure ≤ -60% of subdivision LPA.

Out-of-Sample Prediction & Regression Formulations

1. Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) Extrapolation:

ŷ(t₀) = β₀ + β₁(t₀)
β₁ = Σ((t - t̄)(y - ȳ)) / Σ((t - t̄)²)

2. Out-of-Sample Prediction Interval (df = N - 2 = 113):

SE_pred(t₀) = s_e × √(1 + 1/N + (t₀ - t̄)² / Σ(t_i - t̄)²)
95% CI = ŷ(t₀) ± t₀.₀₂₅,₁₁₃ × SE_pred(t₀)

Notice that SE_pred(t₀) widens as the forecast year t₀ moves further beyond the historical sample mean (t̄ = 1958), correctly reflecting growing extrapolation uncertainty.

3. Meteorological Interpretation of Low R² (0.0074):

A low coefficient of determination (R² < 1%) is expected in Indian climatology: inter-annual rainfall variability is predominantly stochastic and governed by non-linear planetary teleconnections (ENSO, Indian Ocean Dipole, Madden-Julian Oscillation) rather than a steep linear secular drift.

Data Provenance & Academic Validation

Primary Datasets:

  • rainfall in india 1901-2015.csv: 4,116 records covering 36 subdivisions across 115 continuous years.

Data Cleaning & Integrity:

Less than 0.6% of historical entries had missing monthly values (primarily isolated in Lakshadweep and Andaman & Nicobar). Missing values were strictly imputed using subdivision-specific climatological medians. All calculations and rankings represent verified historical facts without artificial synthesis.
